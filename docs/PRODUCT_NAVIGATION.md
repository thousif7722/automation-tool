# Instagram Automation OS - Navigation Map & Route Hierarchy

## Information Architecture Map

```
CUSTOMER DASHBOARD (apps/web)
├── 1. HOME (/home)
│   ├── Business Performance Cards
│   ├── AI Resolution vs Human Escalations Chart
│   └── Top Automations Grid
│
├── 2. AUTOMATION (/automation)
│   ├── My Automations (/automation)
│   ├── Create Choice (/automation/new -> AI Builder vs Visual Builder)
│   ├── AI Workflow Generator (/automation/ai-builder)
│   ├── Visual React Flow Builder (/automation/builder)
│   └── Templates Library (/automation/templates)
│
├── 3. INBOX (/inbox)
│   ├── Filter Tabs (All, Instagram, AI Active, Human Takeover, Assigned)
│   ├── Message Thread Panel
│   └── Customer CRM Sidebar (Lead score, Intent, Tags, Actions)
│
├── 4. CONTACTS (/contacts)
│   ├── All Contacts List
│   ├── Lead Kanban Pipeline (NEW -> QUALIFIED -> CONVERTED)
│   ├── Tags Manager
│   └── Custom Fields Config
│
├── 5. INSTAGRAM (/instagram)
│   ├── Account Health & OAuth Status
│   ├── Comment Monitor
│   ├── DM Monitor
│   └── Story & Mention Monitor
│
├── 6. AI AGENT STUDIO (/ai)
│   ├── Agent Configuration & Brand Voice
│   ├── Knowledge Base (FAQ & Document RAG)
│   └── Escalation Policy Settings
│
├── 7. CONTENT (/content)
│   ├── Post Ideas & Captions
│   ├── Content Calendar
│   └── Scheduled Publishing
│
├── 8. ANALYTICS (/analytics)
│   ├── Revenue & Conversion Attribution
│   ├── Automation Performance
│   └── Natural Language AI Analytics Chat
│
├── 9. TEAM (/team)
│   ├── Workspace Members
│   ├── Roles & Permissions
│   └── Assignment Routing Rules
│
└── 10. SETTINGS (/settings)
    ├── Business Profile
    ├── Instagram Accounts
    ├── Billing & Entitlements
    └── Security Audit Logs
```
